This page describes how the ZenBlock extension handles data. ZenBlock is an ad- and
tracker-blocking browser extension distributed through the Chrome Web Store.

1. DATA WE COLLECT
ZenBlock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It
does not require an account, does not use cookies for tracking, and does not contain
analytics or advertising code.

2. WHAT THE EXTENSION READS AND WHERE IT STAYS
- The extension needs permission to read the URLs of the network requests triggered
  by the pages you visit in order to decide which requests to block. This evaluation
  happens locally on your device, inside the browser.
- Settings you choose (which filter lists are enabled, whitelisted sites, update
  frequency, theme) and simple statistics (how many requests were blocked) are stored
  locally on your device using Chrome's extension storage. This data never leaves
  your browser and is removed if you uninstall or clear data for the extension.

3. NETWORK CONNECTIONS THE EXTENSION MAKES
- The extension downloads public filter lists (EasyList) over HTTPS from the
  well-known official sources for those lists, on installation and then automatically
  on the schedule you choose (default: every 7 days). Only the list files are
  downloaded; nothing about your browsing is sent.
- The extension performs no other network activity.

4. USE OF CHROME PERMISSIONS
- declarativeNetRequest: required to install the blocking rules that stop ads and
  trackers at the network layer.
- declarativeNetRequestFeedback: required to read the count of requests the rules
  matched, so the Dashboard can show accurate, honest blocking statistics.
- alarms: required to schedule filter-list updates and statistics refreshes in the
  background.
- storage: required to keep your settings and statistics locally on your device.
- host_permissions <all_urls>: required so the blocking rules apply on every website
  you visit, which is the core function of an ad blocker.

5. THIRD PARTIES
ZenBlock does not share data with any third party and does not use any third-party
analytics.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
Statistics and settings remain only on your device. Uninstall the extension (or click
"Clear site data" in the options page) to delete them permanently.

7. CONTACT
For questions about this policy, open an issue on the project's public repository.
